Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Clear expectations help protect both the collector and the seller.

Do you guarantee a sale price?

No. Card prices and demand change. Any minimum price and listing strategy should be agreed before listing.

Do I send cards immediately?

No. First submit an inquiry, review the proposed terms, and wait for explicit acceptance and shipping instructions.

How is the commission calculated?

The proposed rate shown here is 25% of net proceeds after the expenses defined in your written agreement. Confirm exactly which fees are deducted.

When do I get paid?

Payout timing depends on marketplace settlement, returns, and the written agreement. Confirm the timeline before consigning.

Do you accept every collection?

No. Acceptance depends on card types, estimated value, condition, demand, and whether the collection can be handled profitably.
